## What's new in MK PDF 1.3.12

**Editing text**
- Edits in tables stay as one piece of text. Some PDFs fill table cells with runs of invisible spaces, and part of the run used to get mixed into the new words, so they came apart when copied or exported. More edits can now be opened and changed again later.

**Export to Word**
- Arabic on scanned pages comes out right when **Recognize text in scanned pages** is on (Mac). Joined letters such as "لا" were turned into other characters before.
- Logos and pictures keep their full colour. Only pictures with text on top of them, like banners and scanned pages, are placed behind the text.

**Repair PDF**
- Repaired files keep the meaning of Arabic joined letters, so text still copies and searches correctly (Mac).

Includes everything in 1.3.11.
