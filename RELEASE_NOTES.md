## What's new in MK PDF 1.3.15

**Scans with Arabic and English on the same line** (Windows, Android, iPhone)
- Recognized lines that mix Arabic and English keep both: the English words and numbers used to be lost from the searchable text, so they couldn't be found, copied or exported. Each part is now written in a font that has its letters.
- No stray hidden characters inside recognized Arabic words.

Scans recognized with an earlier version keep the old text; recognize them again to fix it.

Includes everything in 1.3.14.
