## What's new in MK PDF 1.3.2

**Word export**
- No more empty extra page: the thin line above a page's footer is now the footer's own top border, instead of a tall spacer in the page body that could spill onto a new page.
- Lines that belong to a box (a card's or banner's edges, a chart's gridlines) are no longer added again as separate lines.

Includes everything in 1.3.1: browser-made PDFs stay editable after saving on Mac, and web-style documents (tables with row lines only, banners, cards, charts) export to Word much more faithfully.
